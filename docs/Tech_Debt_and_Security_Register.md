## How to use this register

- **Severity:** Critical (exploitable without login, or data loss across the system) · High (any signed-in user can break a business rule or the server, or a release can fail) · Medium (needs a privileged role, or limited impact) · Low (hygiene, defence in depth).
- **Effort:** S ≤ 1 day · M 2–5 days · L > 1 week.
- **Status:** [Verified] reproduced by a test, command or direct reading · [Inferred] follows from verified facts but not reproduced end to end · [To confirm] needs information outside the repository.
- **Fix now** = a quick win: small, low-risk change with a clear security or integrity benefit.
- **Reproduce** points to a test in `docs/evidence/findings_tests.py` (run from `backend/`: `DJANGO_SETTINGS_MODULE=scratch_settings PYTHONPATH=docs/evidence SCRATCH_MEDIA_ROOT=<temp dir> python manage.py test findings_tests`). A passing test means the finding is present.
- **Owner** is a placeholder until assigned.
